
Container House Group is a manufacturer and exporter of **prefabricated container houses, modular buildings, and accommodation units** for construction sites, mining camps, remote projects, and temporary or permanent facilities.

## Product Range

* 20ft and 40ft container accommodation units
* Prefabricated container homes and site offices
* Expandable container houses (X-Expand)
* Two-story container buildings
* Mining camp accommodation modules
* Remote worker accommodation and site facilities

## Key Features

* Rock wool or PU foam insulation for different climate conditions
* Electrical wiring, lighting, plumbing, doors, and windows
* Factory-finished interior options
* Modular designs for efficient transportation and installation
* Structural calculations and technical drawings
* Export packaging and shipping documentation

## Applications

Container-based buildings can be configured for a range of project requirements, including:

* Mining camps
* Construction sites
* Remote workforce accommodation
* Temporary offices
* Site facilities
* Emergency and temporary housing
* Infrastructure and industrial projects

## Customization

Projects can be configured according to site conditions, climate, layout, occupancy, and local requirements. Available options may include different insulation systems, room layouts, sanitary facilities, electrical configurations, finishes, and exterior designs.

## Manufacturing & Export

Container House Group supports international projects with factory production, technical documentation, quality checks, export packaging, and logistics coordination.
