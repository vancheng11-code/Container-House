Container houses can be configured for many different applications rather than following one fixed layout.

A basic module can be designed as an office, bedroom, living space, storage room, classroom, or temporary accommodation unit.

Interior layouts can also be adjusted according to the project. Buyers may specify doors, windows, partitions, bathrooms, kitchens, and other facilities.

Exterior finishes and colors can be customized to suit residential, commercial, or industrial environments.

For larger projects, multiple modules can be combined into different layouts.

The important step is to define the intended use before production. A site office has different requirements from worker accommodation or a holiday cabin.

This allows the manufacturer to select appropriate insulation, electrical systems, interior finishes, and other components.
